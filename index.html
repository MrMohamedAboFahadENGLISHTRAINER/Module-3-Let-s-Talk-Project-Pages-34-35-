2. Grammar Focus (LOOK! Box)

Master requesting food, quantifiers (a few vs a little), and sense verbs.

Polite Requests

Would you like...?

Offer: Would you like some apples?

Response: Yes, please. I'd like a few apples.

Quantifiers

A few vs. A little

🍏 A few: with countable nouns (apples, coconuts).

🧃 A little: with uncountable nouns (juice, sugar).

Sense Verbs

Sense Verb + Adjective

It / They + taste / look / sound / smell + adjective.

Examples: They look delicious! It smells great! It tastes sour!

Quick Quantifier Check:

1. There are [?] apples in the basket.
2. There's [?] orange juice in the glass.

4. Project & Writing Tip (Page 35)

Asking for help and giving advice in text messages.

Betty & Julie Tablet Screen
Betty: Hi, Julie! I have a problem with my sister. She always takes my clothes, but she never asks me first. Last week she took my favorite sweater. There's a hole in it now! What should I do? 😟
Julie: Hi, Betty! I understand your problem. I think you should talk to your sister. Tell her to ask you before she borrows your things. You can borrow her things, too.
Fred & Harry Phone Screen
Fred: I need your help! My brother got a new tablet. I wanted to play a game, so I took it after I finished my homework. While I was playing, I dropped it and the screen broke. I'm in trouble! Can you help me, please?
Harry: You should apologize to your brother and explain what happened. You can also fix it for him. Don't worry, Harry! It's going to be all right.

Writing Tip: Match Text Message Expressions

Select the correct function for each text message phrase:

1. "I have a problem with my sister... What should I do?"
2. "I think you should talk to your sister."
3. "Don't worry! It's going to be all right."
